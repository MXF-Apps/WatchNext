# Support

- **Something broken?** Open an issue with the bug report template. Include your iOS version, the Sonarr, Radarr and Jellyfin versions, and the relevant lines from **Settings → Diagnostics** in the app (credentials are never logged).
- **Feature idea?** Open an issue and describe the situation it solves.
- **Setup help**: the README walks through configuring the three servers and adding the widget.

WatchNext is a hobby project maintained in spare time. Replies may take a few days.
