# Privacy Policy

WatchNext connects only to the Sonarr, Radarr and Jellyfin servers you configure.

- **Server addresses and display preferences** are stored on your device, in the app's shared container so the widget can read them.
- **API keys and the Jellyfin session token** are stored in the iOS Keychain. The Jellyfin password is never stored; it is exchanged once for a session token.
- **Media titles and artwork** fetched from your servers are cached on your device for display in the app and its widgets.
- **Nothing is collected or transmitted** to the developer or to third parties. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and creates no account anywhere.
- **Deleting the app** removes all stored data. Sign out of Jellyfin from its own interface if you want to revoke the session token it issued.

Questions: open an issue on the project repository.
